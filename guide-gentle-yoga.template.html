Gentle Yoga for Headaches, Colds & Everyday Tension

Not every practice needs to start with a reading. Some days you just want something small and gentle for exactly how you're feeling right now — here are a few simple things worth trying.

For a headache

Shoulder and neck rolls — inhale and draw your shoulders up towards your ears, exhale and let them drop back and down. Repeat slowly, then add a few gentle neck rolls. Headaches often sit in tight shoulders more than we realise.

Cat-Cow — hands and knees, alternate arching and rounding your spine with your breath. Slow, unhurried movement through the whole back of the body.

Savasana — lie flat, arms relaxed, eyes closed. Even five quiet minutes of normal, gentle breathing can ease a headache that's really about needing to switch off.

For a blocked nose or heavy head

Gentle forward fold — feet hip-width apart, soft knees, fold forward and let your head hang. Gravity does some of the work here, easing pressure in the sinuses. Come up slowly.

Child's Pose — kneel, sit back on your heels, fold forward with your forehead resting down (a cushion under your forehead or chest if that's more comfortable). Slow, easy breathing through the nose if you can manage it.

For tension you're carrying

Cat-Cow, again — genuinely useful for more than one thing. Slow spinal movement releases tension that builds up from sitting still or carrying stress in the body.

Legs-Up-The-Wall — lie on your back, legs resting up a wall, arms relaxed at your sides. A quietly restorative pose that needs nothing more than a wall and a few spare minutes.

Keep all of this gentle — nothing here should involve strong twists, deep backbends, or inversions like headstands, especially with a headache or while unwell. If a pose doesn't feel right, stop. This is a starting point for feeling a little better, not medical advice, and it's not a substitute for seeing a doctor if something persists or feels serious.
