Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Who we are

Soul & Selenite is a UK-based small business. For any question about your data, contact us at soulandselenite@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

When you take the crystal quiz, we ask for your email address and, optionally, your first name. We do not collect any other personal information, and the quiz itself does not require you to identify yourself before you see your result — only if you choose to have your full reading emailed to you.

Why we collect it

We use your email address solely to send you the personalised crystal reading you requested through the quiz. We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or any other communication unless you separately and explicitly ask us to.

Legal basis

We process your email address on the basis of legitimate interest — you have actively requested a specific reading, and we send you exactly that, once. If this ever changes (for example, if we introduce a mailing list), we will ask for your clear, separate consent first.

Who we share it with

We use the following third-party services to run this site and send your reading. Each only processes the minimum data needed to do its job:

  • EmailJS — delivers the reading email to your inbox.
  • Hostinger — hosts this website and may log standard technical data (like IP address) as part of normal web server operation.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — helps us understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, quiz steps reached, general device/location) so we can improve it. This only runs if you accept the optional analytics cookies described below, and it never receives your name, email address, or what you typed into the quiz.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We do not maintain our own database of quiz submissions. Your email address is passed to EmailJS at the moment you submit it, purely to send that one message. EmailJS may retain a short delivery history as part of its own service operation — see EmailJS's privacy policy for details.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. Contact soulandselenite@gmail.com for any of these. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use this site — but only if you accept the optional cookies banner shown when you first visit. If you accept, Google Analytics sets a couple of first-party cookies (typically named _ga and _ga_<id>) to distinguish visitors and sessions; if you decline or don't respond, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data about your visit is collected. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site, which will show the banner again on your next visit.

We don't use any advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking cookies. The only other "cookie-like" storage on this site is your browser's own localStorage, used to remember your cookie choice — this never leaves your device.

Marketing emails

The free reading you request through the quiz is always sent regardless of any marketing preference — we never make it conditional on agreeing to receive anything else. Separately, if we ever offer an optional sign-up for occasional Soul & Selenite emails (new content, seasonal offers), it will be a clearly separate, unticked checkbox that you choose for yourself, and every such email will include an easy way to unsubscribe.

We use optional analytics cookies to see how visitors use this site, so we can improve it. No quiz answers, names or email addresses are ever included. Learn more.
